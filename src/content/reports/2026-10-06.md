## Learning Segment

Today is another lesson in catalyst-driven momentum vs. valuation. BABA (+4.68% with volume 51% above average) shows how a China value name can spike on positive flow, while TSLA and NVDA keep pressing records on fundamental and AI narratives. But rich multiples persist across the leaders — AMD at 161 P/E, PLTR at 163, CRWD at 4544 — so the spread is wide between index momentum and the value names like GOOGL (17.2) or TCS.NS (15.0). The tradeable takeaway: follow volume confirmation (as with BABA) rather than chasing price alone, and size positions against stretched valuations.
