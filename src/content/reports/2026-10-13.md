## Learning Segment

Today is a lesson in index-level calm masking single-name violence. The S&P 500 was essentially flat (-0.06%), yet CRWD fell -4.37% and SYM -4.34% while META rose +3.38% — a ~8-point spread inside one session. This is why sector and single-name analysis matters more than the headline index print. Note the valuation dispersion too: TCS.NS trades at 15.7 P/E and rose +4.00%, while CRWD at 4383.5 P/E fell -4.37%. High-multiple names carry more air beneath them when flows reverse. The tradeable takeaway: use volume to confirm moves (META's +3.38% came with volume 78% above average — real participation), and treat the index as a backdrop, not the signal.
