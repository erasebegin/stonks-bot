## Learning Corner: Volume Spikes as a Signal

**What is volume analysis?** Volume measures how many shares traded in a given period. A **volume spike** means significantly more shares changed hands than average.

**When you see a volume spike:**
- **Price up + volume spike** = Strong institutional accumulation. Bulls are in control. (IREN today ✓)
- **Price down + volume spike** = Heavy distribution. Institutions are selling. (MRVL today — but could be rotation, not panic)
- **Price flat + volume spike** = Battle between buyers and sellers. Expect a breakout soon.

**Today's examples:**
- **IREN** (+13.48%, 102% above avg): Textbook accumulation. Institutions adding positions on the data center pivot thesis.
- **MRVL** (-4.59%, 92% above avg): Distribution, but in context of market-wide semi rotation. Watch if it finds support.
- **SNOW** (pre-market, 107% above avg): Earnings-driven accumulation. The gap up is real.

**Rule of thumb:** Don't chase the first volume spike. Wait for confirmation at the next support level. IREN needs to hold $65. MRVL needs to reclaim $205.
