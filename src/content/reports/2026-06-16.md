## 📚 Learning Corner: Volume Analysis

**Today's lesson — Volume spikes tell the real story.**

AA traded at 172% of its average volume while dropping -6.7%. What does that tell us?

**Key principles:**
- **High volume + price drop = distribution** (institutions selling) — AA is clearly seeing institutional rotation out
- **High volume + price surge = accumulation** (institutions buying) — MRVL's +10.4% was probably backed by strong volume too
- **The opposite is true too** — low-volume moves are less meaningful

**For your portfolio:** When you see a holding like AA spike in volume on a bad day, don't panic-sell. Ask: "Is this a fundamental problem or a macro rotation?" If it's macro (like the peace deal causing commodity rotation), the selling is temporary.

**Next step:** Watch AA tomorrow — if volume normalizes and it stabilizes in the $63-65 range, the rotation is done and it's a buying opportunity.
