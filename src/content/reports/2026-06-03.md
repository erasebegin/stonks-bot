## Learning Corner

### Custom ASICs vs. GPUs: What's the Difference?

You hear these terms constantly in AI investing, but what's the practical difference?

**GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)** — NVIDIA's bread and butter. Think of a GPU as a general-purpose parallel processor. It's a Swiss Army knife — it can do many different AI tasks reasonably well. GPUs are great for **training** massive AI models because they can handle the variety of math operations needed.

**ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit)** — A chip designed for exactly one job. Google's TPU, Amazon's Trainium, Marvell's custom designs for AWS. Think of an ASIC as a custom tool — it does one thing extremely efficiently but can't do anything else.

**Why this matters for investors:**
- **Training → GPUs (NVDA wins).** Training giant models like GPT-5 requires flexible computation. NVIDIA dominates here.
- **Inference → ASICs (MRVL, AVGO, GOOGL win).** Once a model is trained, running it ("inference") is a much simpler, repetitive task. ASICs do this at lower cost and power.
- **The shift we're seeing:** As AI moves from training the big models to deploying them everywhere (inference), the ASIC makers benefit disproportionately. This is the MRVL story.
