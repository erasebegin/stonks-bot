## Learning Corner: Why Tech Rotations Happen

When the broader market is flat (-0.26% on the S&P 500) but tech is getting hammered (-0.97% on Nasdaq), that's a **sector rotation**. Money flows out of high-flying sectors (tech, semis) into lagging sectors (financials, energy, healthcare, materials).

**What triggers it:**
- **Profit-taking** after a big run (tech was up 15-20% YTD)
- **Macro uncertainty** (CPI tomorrow creates an "ahead of data" pause)
- **Valuation concerns** (some AI stocks at 100x+ P/E get trimmed first)

**Why it's often healthy:** Rotations redistribute risk and prevent a single sector from becoming a bubble. The money doesn't leave the market — it just moves. When tech stabilizes, it flows back.
