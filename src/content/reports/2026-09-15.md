## News Highlights

- **AI slowdown calls hit chips hard:** Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, Sam Altman, and Elon Musk all publicly backed slowing frontier-model development. The shockwave hit fab-equipment order books: ASML -7.25%, Applied Materials and Lam Research -6%, Soitec ~-13%. Nvidia, Intel, SK Hynix and SoftBank were also hit.
- **Sharp rotation to software/cybersecurity:** As billions shifted out of chips, enterprise software and cybersecurity soared — CrowdStrike +13.85%, UiPath +8%, C3.ai +7%, SoundHound +5%. Asana, Tenable, Pegasystems and Guidewire also rallied. This is a sector rotation, not a broad correction.
- **ASML deeper picture:** Despite Monday's drop, ASML is exploring producing **110+ EUV machines** in 2028 as AI demand stays "very very strong" (JPMorgan). One Wall Street analyst even argues ASML can return ~70% from here. High-NA adoption advances the roadmap but customers must justify ~$400M machine bills.
- **EUCLYD raises €200M+:** The German AI startup raised a Series A of over €200 million, co-led by Samsung, Somerset Capital Partners and Scaleup Europe Fund, targeting the "AI efficiency wall."
- **Samsung power saga:** Samsung rejected a massive ~$18.7 billion electricity prepayment proposal as Korea confronts the power demands of expanding semiconductor production.

## Sentiment

Mixed with a strong rotation under the hood. Equities were broadly lower, but the story isn't a risk-off move — it's a deliberate rotation from AI hardware/chips into software and cybersecurity after top AI CEOs called for a slowdown in development. Watch whether the chip weakness persists or buyers step back in on the ASML "70% return" thesis.
