## 🌙 Weekend Briefing — Monday, 13 July 2026

### 🚨 U.S.-Iran Escalation Over the Weekend

The biggest story heading into Monday's open: **U.S. military strikes against ~140 Iranian targets** over the weekend, following Iran's attack on a container ship in the Strait of Hormuz. Iran has retaliated with strikes against U.S. positions.

**Market implications:**
- **Oil surging** — Brent crude near $76/barrel, up ~5% from pre-escalation levels. The Strait of Hormuz disruption remains the key variable.
- Energy sector (XLE) likely to open strong. CEG could benefit from energy price tailwinds.
- Broader market may face risk-off pressure, particularly on transport and consumer discretionary names sensitive to oil prices.
- Defense stocks likely to see inflows.

This is an escalation of the existing U.S.-Iran conflict — not a new war, but a significant deterioration of the ceasefire that had held for weeks.

### 🇨🇳 Meta Forced to Unwind Manus Deal

Chinese regulators ordered Meta to **unwind its $2B acquisition of agentic AI startup Manus**, classifying the technology as a "sovereign asset." Tencent is in talks to become the largest shareholder.

**Market implications:**
- META surged +5.97% on Friday before this news broke — some pullback possible as the deal reversal introduces uncertainty around Meta's AI M&A strategy.
- The broader signal: China is tightening control over AI intellectual property, which could affect other U.S.-China tech deals going forward.
- Minimal direct portfolio impact but worth monitoring as a regulatory signal.
