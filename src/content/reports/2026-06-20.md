## 📚 Learning Corner

### Volume Spikes as Signal Detector

This week had some extraordinary volume numbers. Let's talk about what they mean.

**Normal volume = no new information.** When a stock trades average volume, the market is in equilibrium — buyers and sellers agree on price.

**Above-average volume = conviction.** When MRVL trades 375% of normal volume, that's institutions making large, deliberate moves. They're not day trading — they're rebalancing, accumulating, or hedging.

**Volume + price direction tells the story:**
- **High volume + price up** → bullish conviction (MRVL ✅)
- **High volume + price down** → bearish distribution (INFY at 283% vol, -9.66%)
- **Low volume + price up** → weak rally, could reverse
- **Low volume + price down** → apathy, not panic (SNOW)

**Key takeaway:** For your portfolio, the volume divergence is clear. Money is flowing INTO semis (MRVL, AVGO) and utilities (CEG). It's flowing OUT of or ignoring materials (AA) and lagging tech (SNOW, MSFT).

When you see volume spikes, check if they're accompanied by institutional filings (13F/13D) a few weeks later. That's where the smart money gets caught.
