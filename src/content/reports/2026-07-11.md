## 📰 Top Stories

### 🍎 Apple Sues OpenAI — Trade Secret Bombshell
Reddit is alight with this one. Apple has filed a lawsuit against OpenAI alleging trade secret theft "at every level" of the organization. This follows the broader tension between Apple and the AI world. The suit claims the scheme was pervasive and systematic. Both r/wallstreetbets (+8.6k) and r/stocks (+1k) have it as the #2 post. This could have major implications for the AI landscape depending on how it shakes out.

### 🇨🇳 China Bans Helium Exports
China temporarily halted helium exports as US-Iran tensions flare again (+1.6k on WSB). Helium is critical for semiconductor manufacturing, MRI machines, and research. With semis already under pressure (MRVL -3.04%), this adds another supply chain concern.

### 🔥 Oil Shocks & Geopolitics
Renewed US-Iran hostilities and attacks on commercial vessels sent crude oil sharply higher, pressuring transportation, housing, materials, and other economically sensitive industries. This is the undercurrent shaping the "how is the market still at ATH?" sentiment on WSB.

### 🏭 SK Hynix Record $26.5B ADR Offering
The memory giant priced a record Nasdaq ADR at $149/share, with the chairman declaring AI demand for memory is "growing exponentially." Bullish signal for the broader AI/semi ecosystem.

### 🤖 Mark Zuckerberg's Meta Chip Play
Meta's newest in-house AI chip enters production in September. This is a long-term story about reducing reliance on external suppliers (NVDA) and building vertical integration. META +5.97% on the week.

### 🔮 Palantir CEO Calls Out OpenAI & Anthropic
Alex Karp went on TV and said "something has gone completely wrong" with OpenAI and Anthropic, arguing enterprises are done paying for tokens. Sent Palantir stock up 8%. Fits with the theme of enterprise AI shifting toward custom solutions.
