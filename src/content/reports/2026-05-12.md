## 📚 Learning Corner: GPU Rental Economics 101

**What are GPU rentals?** Companies like Lambda Labs, Vast.ai, and RunPod let you rent GPU compute by the hour. Think AWS EC2 but for AI training/inference.

**Why rental prices matter:**
- **Spot market** = short-term, surplus capacity. Highly volatile.
- **Reserved/contract pricing** = what enterprises actually pay. Much stickier.
- **Price drops can mean:** oversupply (bearish) OR efficiency gains making compute cheaper (bullish for AI adoption)

**The key metric to watch:** Not the absolute rental price, but the *spread* between spot and contract pricing. A widening spread suggests real oversupply. A narrowing spread suggests demand catching up.

**Why this is different from dot-com:** In 2000, fiber was laid with no one using it. Today, AI models have actual paying customers — ChatGPT, Copilot, enterprise AI tools. The utilization question is about *when*, not *if*.
