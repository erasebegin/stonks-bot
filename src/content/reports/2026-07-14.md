# Stonks Daily — Tuesday, 14 July 2026

## Market Overview

Markets opened the week in the red, with the NASDAQ taking the hardest hit (-1.55%) as a broad chip selloff dragged tech lower. The S&P 500 lost 0.79%, while European and Indian benchmarks held relatively flat.

The story of the day is **semiconductors**: SK Hynix posted a record one-day plunge following its Nasdaq debut, and the shockwaves rippled through the entire sector. AMD (-4.21%), MRVL (-7.75%), AVGO (-3.98%), and ASML (-3.97%) all took heavy losses, while NVDA slipped 3.52%. TSMC's blowout quarter (+36% sales growth) stood in stark contrast — suggesting AI hardware demand is still strong, but the market is rotating and reassessing valuations.

On the positive side, MSFT (+1.53%) bucked the trend, SNOW jumped 2.75%, and PLTR gained 2.56%. INFY surged 5.12% on no obvious catalyst, while CEG (+2.46%) continued its quiet run as an AI-adjacent energy play.
