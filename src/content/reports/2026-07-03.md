## What Happened

**🇺🇸 Jobs Miss Rattles Markets**
The US added just 57,000 jobs in June — well below the ~190K consensus. Unemployment rose to 4.2%. The soft labor data reignited recession whispers and rate-cut speculation, but markets couldn't decide which way to trade it.

**🤖 Palantir CEO Melts Down on Live TV**
Alex Karp had what's being called a "televised nervous breakdown" during a live interview, shouting: *"These models have been completely over, irresponsibly over-sold!"* The clip went viral — 168K upvotes on r/technology, 4.5K on WSB. Karp's rant about AI being oversold is ironic given PLTR's own premium valuation (P/E 145), but the stock actually rose 2.84% on the day. Meanwhile, Spain banned Palantir from state systems and the NHS in the UK is reportedly considering ditching them too.

**🚗 Tesla: Beat Deliveries, Tank Anyway**
Tesla delivered 480,126 vehicles in Q2, beating expectations. Stock dropped 7.49% anyway — classic sell-the-news, possibly amplified by the broader semi/AI weakness. TSLA volume was 64% above average, suggesting heavy institutional rotation.

**🧠 OpenAI Courts the White House**
OpenAI reportedly offered the Trump administration a 5% stake in the company. On WSB, this was met with 5.4K upvotes and heavy skepticism. OpenAI is also reportedly eyeing a 2027 IPO at a $1T valuation.

**🔧 Marvell Technology - Worst in Class**
MRVL cratered 9.84%, leading semi decliners. No specific company news — appears to be caught in the broader AI/semi rotation fear. AVGO (-2.41%) and ASML (-4.00%) also felt the heat.

**💻 Apple's Memory Solution Boosts Stock**
AAPL jumped 4.84% after suggesting cheap Chinese chips could resolve the RAM price crisis. Strong session, breaking the week's bearish pattern for big tech.

**🇪🇺 Google Loses EU Antitrust Fight**
The EU's top court upheld Google's record €4.3B ($4.7B) antitrust fine. GOOGL only slipped 0.36%, suggesting the market had already priced this in.
