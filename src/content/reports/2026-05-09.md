## 📚 Learning Corner: Breakouts & Support/Resistance

Intel's +25% move is a textbook breakout. Here's how to read it:

**Resistance becomes support:** INTC had been stuck in a trading range. The ceiling of that range (~$25-30) is now the new floor. If the stock pulls back to that level and holds, that's a very strong buy signal — the market is confirming "this is the new fair value."

**Volume confirms conviction:** A breakout on low volume is suspicious (could be manipulation). Intel's move came on massive volume (WSJ exclusive, institutional rebalancing). This is real money, not retail FOMO.

**The gap-fill risk:** Stocks that gap up dramatically often "fill the gap" — retrace part of the move as early profit-takers exit. Don't be alarmed if INTC gives back 5-10% early next week. That's healthy consolidation, not a reversal signal.

**Key levels to watch on INTC:** The pre-breakout range was ~$20-28. The stock closed Friday ~$35. Support at $28 (old resistance), then $25. If it breaks below $20, the thesis is wrong.
