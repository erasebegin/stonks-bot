## 📚 Learning Corner: AI Infrastructure Capex & the "Build vs. Buy" Dynamic

**What is "Build vs. Buy"?** In enterprise tech, companies face a fundamental choice: build a solution in-house (custom hardware, proprietary software) or buy it from third-party vendors (Nvidia GPUs, AWS cloud, etc.).

**Why it matters for investors:** When hyperscalers like Microsoft or Google announce they're building more in-house, it changes the competitive landscape:
- **Bullish for:** The companies that supply the raw materials and chips they do buy (ASML, TSMC, MRVL for connectivity)
- **Bearish for:** The companies whose products they now make internally (NVDA faces risk if MSFT builds its own AI chips)

**The key insight:** Total AI spend is exploding ($700B+), so even if hyperscalers capture a larger share internally, the absolute spend on external vendors can still grow. The pie is getting big enough that everyone can eat — just not at the same rate as before.

Look for this tension to play out in earnings calls throughout 2026. When a hyperscaler says "we're optimizing our silicon strategy," what they really mean is "we're building in-house to capture more margin."
