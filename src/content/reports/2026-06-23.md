## 📚 Learning Corner

### Understanding P/E Contractions in a Sector Rotation

When the Russell 2000 hits record highs while the Nasdaq drops, it's a "rotation" — money moving from growth/tech to value/small-cap. This compresses P/E multiples for growth stocks **even when nothing changed about the company**.

Example: AVGO dropped 4.67% on Monday but announced a $2.5B buyback last week — no negative news. The stock didn't become 4.67% worse as a business. The market simply re-rated the multiple investors are willing to pay.

This is why monitoring "price vs. value" matters: a good company at a better price is an opportunity, not a warning.
