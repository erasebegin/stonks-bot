## Weekend Briefing — Monday, 25 May 2026

### 🏆 Geopolitical: US & Iran Agree to Reopen Strait of Hormuz

The biggest weekend development: the US and Iran have agreed **in principle to reopen the Strait of Hormuz**, per a US official. This follows what had been escalating tensions and conflict in the region.

**Market implications:**
- **Brent crude fell ~5%** on the news — already pricing in the resolution
- **Bearish for oil stocks & energy ETFs** in the near term
- **Bullish for global equities** — one of the biggest geopolitical risk factors removed
- **Positive for shipping, airlines, and import-dependent economies** (India, Japan, EU)
- Portfolio notes: AA (Alcoa) and SCCO (Southern Copper) could benefit from reduced risk premium on materials. CEG (Constellation Energy) less directly affected but broader risk-on sentiment helps.
