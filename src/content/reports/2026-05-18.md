# 🌙 Weekend Briefing — Monday, 18 May 2026

Two notable developments brewing over the weekend that could set the tone for Monday's open.

---

## 🔴 1. Samsung Strike — 50k Workers Walk

**Samsung dropped 8.6%** as 40% of its South Korean workforce (50k employees) prepares for an **18-day strike** over profit-sharing demands.

| Detail | |
|---|---|
| Workers affected | 50k (40% of SK workforce) |
| Daily loss risk | $2B |
| Duration | 18 days |
| Demand | 15% profit share |

**Market implications:**
- **Bullish for memory competitors** — MU, WDC, STX could benefit from a supply squeeze
- DRAM ETF (DRAM) has 20% Samsung, 27% SK Hynix exposure
- Semi sector may see rotation toward non-Samsung-linked names
- Key question: supply squeeze vs demand slowdown?

**Portfolio relevance:** Semis are your biggest sector (MRVL, AVGO, ASML). While Samsung is a consumer/producer rather than a direct competitor, a memory supply shock could lift the whole semi space in the short term.

---

## 🔴 2. Gold & Silver — Friday's Collapse

Gold saw its **biggest single-day drop in recent memory**: $4,652 → $4,538 (low $4,511 intraday). Silver cratered from $88 to $75.89.

**Context:** CPI at 3.8%, PPI at 6%. The bond market is sending a signal the Fed isn't verbalizing yet.

**Portfolio relevance:**
- **SCCO** (Southern Copper) — copper follows broader metals sentiment; could see pressure
- **AA** (Alcoa) — aluminum may be affected by the risk-off metals move
- **SPLT.L** (Platinum ETC) — direct precious metals exposure
- These are long-term commodity plays; Friday's move is a sharp correction in a strong uptrend, not a reversal thesis
