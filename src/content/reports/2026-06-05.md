## 📚 Learning Corner — Understanding the Tech Rotation

**What is a "sector rotation"?**

Sector rotation is when institutional money flows from one market sector to another based on the macroeconomic cycle. Think of it like a game of musical chairs — when one sector becomes too expensive (or the economic outlook changes), fund managers sell and move into sectors that are expected to benefit from the *next* phase.

**Right now we're seeing a rotation from:**

| From (Out of favor) | To (In favor) |
|---|---|
| Technology / Semis | Financials, Healthcare |
| Growth (high P/E) | Value (low P/E) |
| Momentum-driven AI plays | Cyclicals tied to economic recovery |

**Why does it happen?** Because the AI/semiconductor trade has been the dominant theme for 18+ months. When a single sector gets too crowded, the "smart money" takes profits and redeploys into areas that haven't run yet. Banks (benefiting from higher-for-longer rates) and healthcare (defensive + AI drug discovery tailwinds) are natural recipients.

**The takeaway:** Rotations are healthy, not bearish. They reset valuations and create buying opportunities in secular winners (like Broadcom at -12%). The key is distinguishing between a *rotation* (tactical rebalancing) and a *regime change* (structural shift). This looks like a rotation.
