## Learning Segment

Today is a lesson in reading a quiet tape correctly. Every major index moved less than 0.6%, yet INFY (+5.26%), AI (+3.45%) and META (+2.63%) were up sharply while ASML (-1.86%) and PPLT (-1.44%) fell — again, index-level calm hiding single-name dispersion. Notice the valuation signal too: INFY at 13.4 P/E and TCS.NS at 15.2 P/E led the tape (both cheap), while CRWD at a 5224 P/E and PLTR at 179.9 P/E went nowhere. Cheap names ran, expensive names stalled — when flows return to a sector, the lower-multiple leaders often move first because there is less air beneath them. The tradeable takeaway: on days when the index says "nothing happened," rank the movers by P/E and see which side of the valuation divide is winning. That tells you more than the headline.
