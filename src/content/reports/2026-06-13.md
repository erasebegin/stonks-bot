## 📚 Learning Corner: Quad Witching — The Hidden Force

**What is it?** The third Friday of March, June, September, and December — when stock index futures, stock index options, single stock futures, and single stock options all expire on the same day. This June 19 is one of those days.

**Why it matters:**
- ~$5 trillion in notional options/futures exposure rolls over
- Market makers need to hedge delta exposure, creating massive volume
- Prices can swing 1-3% in either direction on no real news
- **Average quad witching day: +0.3%** (slightly bullish) but with 2x normal volatility

**What smart money does:**
1. Avoid entering big new positions late Friday — wait for Monday
2. If you have options expiring, close them by Thursday 3 PM to avoid pin risk
3. Use the volatility to set limit orders at support/resistance levels
4. Don't chase the move — quad witching moves are mechanical, not fundamental

**Portfolio impact:** MRVL inclusion means added buying pressure regardless of the quadruple witching noise. The two events overlap on June 19-22, creating a unique setup where mechanical buying (index inclusion) may offset witching-driven selling.
