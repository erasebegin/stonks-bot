## 🧠 Learning Corner — Volume Analysis: Reading the Market's Attention

When a stock moves on **unusually high volume**, it tells you something different than a price move on normal volume. Today gives us perfect case studies:

**SNOW (+36.48%, 325% above average volume):** This is institutional conviction. Retail can't move a $70B+ company by 36%. When volume is 3-4x normal and price explodes, you're seeing large funds repositioning. This is a **structural re-rating** signal — not a day trade.

**SYM (-8.99%, 292% above average volume):** The same logic in reverse. 3x normal volume on a 9% drop means institutions are **exiting** positions decisively. This isn't noise. Someone knows something or sentiment has structurally shifted.

**PATH (+3.76%, 107% above avg):** Above-average but not extreme volume on a moderate gain suggests **accumulation** — institutions buying into strength but not urgently. A "steady accumulation" signal.

**The rule:** Price movement + confirming volume = conviction. Price movement alone = could be noise. When volume leads price, it's time to pay attention.
