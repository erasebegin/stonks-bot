## 🧠 Learning Corner: P/E Compression During Sector Rotation

When interest rates rise or sentiment shifts, high-P/E sectors (especially tech/semis) reprice faster than the broader market. This is **P/E compression** — the multiple investors are willing to pay for future earnings shrinks.

Example: MRVL at $279 with P/E 96.2. If the P/E compresses to 70 (still rich), the stock would need to drop to ~$203 to maintain the same earnings — a 27% downside from here. That's the risk baked into these names during rotation.

**Watch the 10Y yield and the dollar** — both are the primary drivers of P/E compression in growth stocks.
