## 📚 Learning Corner

### Relative Strength (Basics)

When the Nasdaq drops 1.15%, a stock going up 2.15% (CEG) shows **relative strength**. When a stock drops 10% (MRVL), it shows **relative weakness**.

You can measure this informally by comparing a stock's % change to the index's % change:

```
Relative Strength = Stock Change % - Index Change %
CEG vs Nasdaq: +2.15% - (-1.15%) = +3.30% RS ✅
MRVL vs Nasdaq: -9.78% - (-1.15%) = -8.63% RS ❌
```

Positive RS means the stock is outperforming. Consistent positive RS over days/weeks is one of the strongest signals for an uptrend.
