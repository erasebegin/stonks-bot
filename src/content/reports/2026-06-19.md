## 📚 Learning Corner

### Event Clustering — When Multiple Catalysts Hit Same Day

Thursday was a rare "double catalyst" day: a sector-specific catalyst (Intel-Apple) AND a macro catalyst (Iran peace) hitting simultaneously. This is called **event clustering**.

**How to read it:**
- **Sector catalyst + macro catalyst in same direction** → amplified move
- **Sector catalyst + macro catalyst in opposite directions** → whipsaw

Thursday was a bullish cluster: the Intel-Apple deal supercharged semis, while the Iran peace deal crushed oil (which lowers input costs for chipmakers indirectly) and boosted risk appetite (VIX -11%).

**When clusters happen:**
- The sector with the strongest idiosyncratic catalyst wins
- Check if the macro catalyst is durable (Iran deal is structural) or temporary (short-term)
- The most impacted portfolio positions are those exposed to BOTH catalysts

Your AVGO + MRVL + ASML were riding BOTH the semi sector wave and the broader risk-on macro wave. That's why they did +4-7% while the S&P only did +1%.
