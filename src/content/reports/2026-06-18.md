## 📚 Learning Corner

### Sector Divergence — What It Tells You

When one sector (semiconductors) goes up while the broad market goes down, that's called **sector divergence**. It's a powerful signal.

**How to read it:**
- **Sector divergence UP while market DOWN** → Rotational buying. Money is flowing out of general stocks INTO that sector. Usually means that sector has a specific catalyst the rest of the market doesn't share.
- **Sector divergence DOWN while market UP** → Rotational selling. The sector is being de-rated.

**June 17 example:** Semis had an idiosyncratic catalyst (Intel 18A-P + AI chip alliance). The broader market sold on macro (Fed hawkishness). When these cross, the sector with the catalyst usually wins in the short term.

**Actionable:** When you see sector divergence, check if the catalyst is:
- **Structural** (new tech, regulatory change, long-term trend) → likely sustainable
- **Tactical** (one-off event, short-term news) → likely fades in 1-3 days

Intel 18A-P is structural. Fed rate hike fears are tactical (they wax and wane with data releases). Semis are the better bet here.
