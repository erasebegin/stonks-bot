## 📚 Learning Corner: RSI (Relative Strength Index)

**What it is:** RSI measures the speed and magnitude of recent price changes to identify overbought (>70) or oversold (<30) conditions.

**How to use it:**
- RSI > 70: Potentially overbought — consider taking profits
- RSI < 30: Potentially oversold — potential entry point
- RSI divergence: Price makes new high but RSI doesn't → weakening momentum

**Caveat:** RSI can stay overbought in strong uptrends and oversold in crashes. Never trade on RSI alone — use it with trend analysis and volume confirmation.
