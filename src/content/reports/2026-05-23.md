## 📚 Learning Corner: Russell Reconstitution

This week, FTSE Russell announced its annual index reconstitution — and **AMD and GOOGL are being removed from the Russell value indexes**. Here's what that means:

**What is Russell Reconstitution?**
Every June, FTSE Russell rebalances its indexes (Russell 1000, 2000, Growth, Value, etc.). Stocks are reshuffled based on their current market cap and valuation characteristics. It's the world's most-watched index shuffle because **trillions of dollars track these indexes**.

**Why does it matter?**
When a stock is removed from a "Value" index, passive value funds (like IWD, IWN) must sell their positions. This creates temporary selling pressure. It also signals that the market now classifies AMD and GOOGL as growth stocks — no surprise given their valuations, but it does mean they lose the "value" buyer base.

**The takeaway:** 
- Being removed from value indexes confirms they've gotten expensive
- But active fund managers who run value strategies might see this as a buying opportunity if they disagree with the classification
- The selling is mechanical, not fundamental — don't panic if you hold these
