### Learning Corner — RSI Divergence

Friday's selloff was preceded by a bearish **RSI divergence** on the SOXX daily chart. The price was making higher highs in late May/early June, but RSI was making lower highs — telling you momentum was weakening before the price collapsed.

**Key takeaway:** RSI divergence doesn't tell you *when* the reversal will happen, but it tells you the trend is losing steam. Combine it with volume analysis (declining volume on up-moves, surging volume on down-moves) for a more complete picture.
