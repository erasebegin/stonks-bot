## 📚 Learning Corner: **Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP)**

VWAP is the "fair price" of a stock for the day, calculated by multiplying every trade price by the volume traded at that price, then dividing by total volume.

**Why it matters:**
- **Institutional benchmark:** Big funds often aim to buy below VWAP and sell above it
- **Support/resistance:** Stocks trading above VWAP are considered "strong"; below VWAP = "weak"
- **Volume confirmation:** Today's heavy volume drops (ASML +78% above avg, BIDU +91%) suggest institutions were big sellers, not just retail panic

**Quick rule:** If a stock gaps down on 2x+ average volume, wait for it to reclaim VWAP before buying. If it can't, the selling may continue. If it reclaims VWAP quickly, that's a strong bounce signal.
