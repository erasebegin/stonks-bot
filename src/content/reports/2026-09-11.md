## 📰 Top Stories

**🟢 AAPL (Apple) Jumps 3.56% to $326.57**
Apple was the standout gainer of the session, bucking the broader tech selloff with a strong move on heavy volume (65% above average). No single headline drove it — the bid looked like rotation out of beaten-down semis and into large-cap quality.

**🔴 AMD, NVDA, ASML Slide as Semis Retreat**

**⚪ META Settles for ~$18B Over Teen Mental-Health Claims**
Meta agreed to pay roughly $18 billion to resolve claims it designed addictive platforms that harmed young people. The settlement removes a major legal overhang but raises questions about future teen engagement. META closed -1.42% at $644.38.
